## 7. Fonts and typography

## 8. AI-generated assets

If an AI service is used to generate an asset, record:

- Service or model.
- Asset description.
- Date generated.
- Applicable terms.
- Whether the asset was modified.
- Whether any external reference material was used.

Do not claim that an AI-generated asset is human-created original work without qualification.

Review generated assets for recognisable copyrighted characters, logos, protected designs, or other problematic material.

## 9. External references

References used for inspiration do not automatically become project assets.

Record important visual or technical references separately.

Do not copy another game's artwork, branding, models, or distinctive protected expression.

Use references to guide original work.

## 12. Final attribution policy

Never guess a creator, licence, source, or attribution requirement.

When information is unknown, mark it as **Unverified** and resolve it before release.

The final public credits must describe the actual materials shipped in Wound Up, not merely the tools considered during development.
